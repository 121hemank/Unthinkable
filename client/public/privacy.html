Privacy Policy

Clinic Appointment Manager — last updated: August 2026

This platform lets patients book doctor appointments and lets doctors manage visits, notes and follow-up reminders. This policy explains what we store and why.

Data we collect

  • Account data — name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Appointment data — booked time slots, status history and cancellation/reschedule events.
  • Clinical data — symptom descriptions you submit, doctor's clinical notes and prescriptions, and AI-generated plain-language summaries of both.
  • Medication reminders — medicine names, dose times and course duration so the system can email you reminders.
  • Google Calendar token — if you link your calendar, we store an OAuth refresh token used only to create, move or delete event entries for your own appointments.

How we use it

Data is used exclusively to operate the clinic workflow: showing you your appointments, preparing pre-visit briefs for your doctor, sending booking, cancellation and medication emails, and syncing confirmed visits to Google Calendar. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Appointment summaries are generated by Google's Gemini model from the text you provide; that text is not used to train models.

Email delivery

Transactional emails are sent through our configured mail service. Your address is never added to marketing lists.

Data removal

Contact us at the clinic email shown in the app footer and we will delete your account and associated records. Disconnecting Google Calendar stops all calendar API usage immediately.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the clinic administrator through the support email listed on the application home page.
